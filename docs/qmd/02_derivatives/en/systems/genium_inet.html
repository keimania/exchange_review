1. System Overview

Item Details
System Name Genium INET
Operated By Nasdaq, Inc. (formerly NASDAQ OMX Group); licensed to 40+ exchanges globally
Purpose Multi-asset class trading and clearing platform for derivatives, equities, fixed income, commodities, and FX
Launch Year 2010 (Genium INET for Nordic derivatives); platform lineage traces to OM/CLICK from 1980s
Current Version Genium INET 8.0.0.0 (released June 2025); successor platform Nasdaq Eqlipse launched 2025
Vendor Nasdaq, Inc. — Market Technology division (Stockholm-based development)

Source: Nasdaq IR, “NASDAQ OMX Launches World’s Fastest Trading System for Its Nordic Derivatives Markets”, October 2010 Source: Nasdaq, “Genium INET Upgrade 8.0.0.0”, 2025

System Evolution

Period System Name Key Changes
1980s–1990s OM CLICK / CLICK Trade OM AB (Swedish options market operator) developed the CLICK electronic trading system for derivatives; widely licensed to Nordic exchanges and beyond
Late 1990s–2003 SAXESS OMX developed SAXESS as the common equity trading platform for the NOREX Alliance (Stockholm, Helsinki, Copenhagen, Oslo, Iceland, Tallinn, Riga); unified Nordic/Baltic cash equity trading
2003–2007 Genium (standalone) OMX Technology developed the Genium platform as a next-generation multi-asset trading and clearing system, incorporating lessons from CLICK and SAXESS
2007–2008 NASDAQ OMX Merger NASDAQ acquired OMX Group (2007), combining NASDAQ’s INET matching engine technology with OMX’s Genium/CLICK derivative platform heritage
2010 Genium INET Launch Combined platform launched October 25, 2010 for NASDAQ OMX Nordic cash and fixed income derivatives; marketed as “world’s fastest trading system” with sub-100 microsecond latency
2011–2013 Global Deployments SGX adopted Genium INET (as “SGX Reach/Titan”); HKEX upgraded HKATS/DCASS to Genium INET (October 2013); ASX adopted for derivatives (ASX Trade24); Borsa Istanbul (BISTECH) also adopted
2014–2024 Maturation Continuous version upgrades (v4.x through v8.x); deployed at NLX (London, 2013–2015), Montreal Exchange (considered), and numerous other venues; Pre-trade Risk Management (PRM/TradeGuard) integrated
2025–Present Nasdaq Eqlipse (4th Generation) Successor platform announced; unified suite spanning trading, clearing, CSD, and data intelligence; microservice architecture; cloud-ready; “evergreen” quarterly upgrade model; Genium INET continues in production at existing deployments during transition

2. Architecture

2.1 High-Level Design

Genium INET follows a modular, event-driven architecture designed to support multiple asset classes (derivatives, equities, fixed income, commodities) within a single integrated platform. Unlike the INET equities matching engine which is optimized for single-threaded pure speed, Genium INET balances performance with the rich functionality required for derivatives markets (implied pricing, complex strategies, pro-rata matching, clearing integration).

Design Principles: - Multi-asset, multi-market — a single platform instance can support multiple products, markets, and asset classes simultaneously - Integrated trading and clearing — designed as a combined trading + clearing solution (unlike many equities systems where clearing is separate) - Configurable matching logic — supports multiple matching algorithms (price-time priority, pro-rata, and hybrid configurations) per product - Real-time risk management — pre-trade and at-trade risk controls embedded in the order processing path - High availability — active-standby architecture with hot standby at secondary site

Data Flow (simplified): 1. Order arrives via gateway (FIX, OUCH, or OMnet API) over TCP 2. Pre-trade risk validation applied (PRM/TradeGuard: price limits, quantity limits, credit checks) 3. Order routed to the appropriate matching engine partition (by product/market) 4. Matching engine executes configured matching algorithm (price-time, pro-rata, or hybrid) 5. For derivatives: implied pricing engine evaluates potential implied orders from strategy combinations 6. Execution reports returned to participant; trade data forwarded to clearing engine (DCASS or equivalent) 7. Market data update published via ITCH (multicast/unicast) and AMD (Advanced Market Data)

2.2 Core Components

Component Function Details

Risk Engine (PRM / TradeGuard) Pre-trade risk management; validates orders against configurable limits before reaching matching engine Fat-finger price checks; maximum order quantity checks; accumulated value limits; net/gross position limits; virtually zero additional latency; supports ESMA guidelines compliance
Surveillance Interface (SMARTS) Real-time feed of all order and trade activity to Nasdaq SMARTS surveillance platform Used by 50+ exchanges and 170+ banks/brokers globally; order book replay capability; AI-powered anomaly detection; market manipulation and insider trading detection

4. Interfaces & Protocols

4.1 Order Entry

Protocol Type Transport Description Use Case
OUCH Native binary SoupBinTCP Simple, high-performance order entry protocol; supports entering, replacing, canceling orders and receiving executions; minimal overhead; designed for integration into proprietary trading systems HFT firms and algorithmic traders seeking lowest latency
FIX (5.0 SP2) Industry standard TCP Financial Information Exchange protocol adapted for Genium INET; standard FIX semantics for order management; broader message set than OUCH Institutional participants, brokers, and firms with existing FIX infrastructure
OMnet API Proprietary binary OMnet middleware Nasdaq’s native proprietary API; provides full access to Genium INET functionality including advanced features; OMnet Gateway Process manages connections for API clients Participants requiring full functionality; exchange operator applications

Key Order Entry Features: - Complex/strategy order entry (multi-leg spreads, straddles, etc.) - Mass quote entry and maintenance for market makers - Request-for-quote (RFQ) submission - Order modification (price and quantity changes) - Cancel-on-disconnect capability - Kill switch (cancel all open orders) - Conformance testing required for all connecting applications before production access

Source: Nasdaq, “Genium INET Protocol Specifications” Source: Nasdaq, “Genium INET OMnet Application Programmer’s Interface Manual”

4.2 Market Data

Protocol Type Transport Description
ITCH (Genium INET ITCH) Native binary MoldUDP64 (multicast) Direct data feed displaying all public orders and trades on auto-matched markets; order-by-order full depth-of-book view; outbound only (does not support order entry); variable-length binary messages; versions vary by deployment (ITCH 2.0 at some venues)
AMD (Advanced Market Data) Derived data MoldUDP64 / SoupBinTCP Provides derived/aggregated market data views (BBO, trade summaries); complementary to ITCH raw order-by-order feed
Glimpse Snapshot SoupBinTCP (unicast) On-demand full order book snapshot service; used for initial state recovery or gap fill; point-to-point request/response

ITCH Key Message Types (Derivatives): - Derivative Order Book Directory — contract specifications, product parameters, implied pricing flags - System Event — market open/close, start/end of trading sessions - Add Order — new order added to the book - Order Executed — partial or full execution - Order Delete — order removed from the book - Trade — executed trade information - Equilibrium Price — auction equilibrium price updates - Implied Price — implied bid/offer prices derived from strategy combinations

Data Encoding: - Binary encoded messages - Variable-length based on message type - Sequenced messages for gap detection and recovery - Nanosecond-precision timestamps

Source: Nasdaq, “Genium INET ITCH Protocol Specification NFX” Source: OnixS, “ITCH Protocol Usage”

4.3 Drop Copy

Protocol Function Description
FIX Drop Copy Real-time execution and order status copies Based on FIX protocol; provides copies of all order activity (entry, modification, execution, cancellation) to designated drop copy sessions; CopyMsgIndicator (797)=“Y” on all copied messages; when orders are entered via OMnet, Execution Reports are sent on the Drop session; cross-protocol visibility enabled

Source: Borsa Istanbul, “Genium INET FIX Drop Copy Protocol Specification”

4.4 Reference Data

Data Type Distribution Method Description
FIX Reference Data FIX 5.0 SP2 Contract specifications, product definitions, trading schedules; based on FIX Application Sequencing; Resend Request supported but responded to with Sequence Reset gap fill; message recovery via application sequencing
ITCH Reference Data ITCH system/directory messages Derivative Order Book Directory messages broadcast before market open; includes tick tables, lot sizes, trading status, implied pricing parameters
OMnet Reference Data OMnet API broadcasts Full reference data access via OMnet middleware; queued broadcasts polled by API clients

Source: Borsa Istanbul, “Genium INET FIX Reference Data Protocol Specification” Source: Nasdaq, “FIX for Genium INET for NASDAQ Nordic 2.0”

5. Resilience & Business Continuity

5.1 Architecture

Aspect Details
Deployment Model Primary site (active) + secondary site (hot standby); central system components run on the primary site with real-time state replication to secondary
Failover Model In the event of a primary site failure, the matching engine on the secondary site is promoted to primary mode; ITCH ports on secondary site become active only after matching engine assumes primary role
Heartbeat Monitoring Primary and secondary systems exchange regular status signals; failure detection triggers predetermined failover procedures
Gateway Redundancy Multiple order entry and market data gateways; participants maintain connections to both primary and backup infrastructure
Network Redundancy Redundant network paths between primary and secondary sites; diverse physical cable routes
Data Integrity Real-time state replication ensures minimal data loss during failover; order book state recoverable from replicated log

5.2 Deployment-Specific Configurations

Each exchange deploying Genium INET configures its own business continuity plan. Examples:

  • Nasdaq Nordic: Primary and secondary data centers with hot standby; detailed BCP published to members; periodic DR failover tests conducted
  • HKEX: HKATS on Genium INET operates with primary site and DR site in Hong Kong; HKEX maintains its own BCP independent of Nasdaq’s standard configuration
  • SGX: Singapore Exchange maintains separate primary and DR facilities with Genium INET-based systems

5.3 Operational Resilience

  • Conformance Testing: All applications connecting to Genium INET must pass conformance testing before production access, ensuring they handle failover scenarios, market state changes, and error conditions correctly
  • Test Environments: Dedicated Genium INET test systems available for participant connectivity testing, conformance certification, and disaster recovery simulation
  • Capacity Planning: Systems designed with significant headroom above normal operating volumes; stress testing conducted regularly

Source: NASDAQ OMX Nordic, “Business Continuity Plan Description” Source: Nasdaq, “Genium INET Test Systems” Source: Nasdaq, “Genium INET Conformance”

6. Notable Technical Features

6.1 Multi-Asset Support

Genium INET is a truly multi-asset platform, supporting equities, equity derivatives, fixed income, fixed income derivatives, commodities (including energy and agricultural), FX derivatives, and index products within a single integrated system. This contrasts with many exchange technology platforms that require separate systems for different asset classes. The platform’s flexibility has made it attractive to exchanges seeking a single technology solution for their entire product range.

6.2 Flexible Matching Algorithms

Unlike pure equities platforms that typically use only price-time priority, Genium INET supports multiple configurable matching algorithms critical for derivatives markets: - Price-Time Priority (FIFO): Standard algorithm where orders at the best price are matched first, with time priority at the same price level; used for most equity and liquid derivative products - Price Pro-Rata (PPR): Priority given to best price, but at the last matching price level, volume is allocated proportionally based on resting order size; commonly used for interest rate futures and options where market maker incentives matter - Hybrid configurations: Exchanges can configure algorithm parameters per product, including minimum allocation sizes and top-of-book priority adjustments

Source: Nasdaq, “Genium INET - Pro-Rata Matching”

6.3 Complex Order / Strategy Support

For derivatives trading, Genium INET provides comprehensive strategy order management: - Pre-defined strategies: Calendar spreads, inter-commodity spreads, straddles, strangles, and other standard strategy types - User-defined strategies: Participants can create custom multi-leg combinations subject to exchange-defined rules - Strategy matching: Native strategy matching engine handles multi-leg executions atomically - Strategy-to-outright interaction: Strategy orders can interact with outright order books through the implied pricing engine

6.4 Implied Pricing

The implied pricing engine is one of Genium INET’s most sophisticated derivatives-specific features: - Implied-in: Generates implied strategy orders from combinations of outright leg orders - Implied-out: Generates implied outright orders from strategy orders combined with other outright orders - Multi-level implication: Supports chains of implied prices across multiple related products - Liquidity enhancement: Implied pricing effectively links outright and strategy order books, increasing visible liquidity and narrowing spreads - Configurable: Exchanges can enable or disable implied pricing per product and configure the depth of implication

6.5 Mass Quote for Market Makers

Efficient market maker support through mass quoting capabilities: - Allows simultaneous submission and update of two-sided quotes across multiple products in a single transaction - Maximum order quantity checks performed per mass quote transaction - Configurable maximum number of quote items per transaction - Quote update throttling to manage message traffic - Delta quote updates for efficient modification of existing quotes

6.6 Surveillance Integration (SMARTS)

Genium INET provides native integration with Nasdaq’s SMARTS surveillance platform: - Real-time feed of all order and trade data to SMARTS - Full order book replay capability for investigation — rebuild the entire order book over any timeframe - Cross-market surveillance across all Genium INET-connected markets - AI-powered anomaly detection for market manipulation, insider trading, and spoofing - Used by over 50 exchanges and 170+ banks/brokers globally - Generative AI features (introduced ~2024) streamline investigative procedures

Source: Nasdaq, “Nasdaq Market Surveillance (SMARTS) Features”

6.7 Pre-Trade Risk Management (PRM / TradeGuard)

Integrated pre-trade risk controls with virtually no additional latency impact: - Fat Finger Price Checks: Compare incoming order prices against reference prices - Maximum Order Quantity: Configurable per participant and product - Accumulated Value Limits: Track and limit total order value over configurable time windows - Position Limits: Net and gross position limit enforcement - Sponsored Access Controls: Supports GCM (General Clearing Member) / NCM (Non-Clearing Member) risk relationships - DMA Controls: Low-latency Direct Market Access validation - ESMA Compliance: Designed to meet EU MiFID II / ESMA requirements for automated trading controls

Source: Nasdaq, “PRE-TRADE RISK MANAGEMENT Product Sheet” Source: Nasdaq, “NASDAQ NORDIC Genium INET Pre-trade Risk Management Service Guide”

Key Differentiators

Genium INET Advantages: - Widely deployed and battle-tested: 40+ exchange deployments across Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and Middle East provide extensive operational track record and continuous improvement from diverse use cases - Modular and configurable design: Exchanges can select matching algorithms, risk parameters, product configurations, and protocol combinations tailored to their specific market structure — a critical advantage for derivatives markets with diverse product types - Proven Asia-Pacific derivatives track record: Major derivatives deployments at HKEX, SGX, and ASX demonstrate the platform’s suitability for high-volume Asian derivatives markets with complex product mixes - Integrated trading and clearing: Combined trading + clearing solution reduces integration complexity and provides consistent risk management across the trade lifecycle - Rich derivatives functionality: Implied pricing, pro-rata matching, complex order support, mass quoting, and flexible strategy definitions represent derivatives-specific features refined over decades from the OM/CLICK heritage - Surveillance ecosystem: Native SMARTS integration provides world-class surveillance capabilities, important for regulatory compliance across jurisdictions

KRX Exture+ Advantages: - Faster raw matching: Exture+’s ≤ 70 μs is competitive with or faster than many Genium INET deployments, reflecting focused optimization for a single market - Unified single-market platform: All KRX asset classes on one system eliminates cross-system coordination complexity - Complete domestic control: In-house development provides full sovereignty over technology decisions, upgrade timing, and customization without vendor dependency - Solace hardware messaging: Purpose-built messaging appliances provide guaranteed, deterministic data delivery - Total cost transparency: No licensing fees to external vendors; development costs borne entirely by KRX/KOSCOM

Architectural Philosophy Differences: - Genium INET is optimized for flexibility and global portability — designed to serve diverse exchanges with different market structures, regulatory requirements, and product mixes, making configurability and multi-asset support paramount - KRX Exture+ is optimized for comprehensive coverage of a single national market — all asset classes, all matching logic, all risk controls tailored specifically for the Korean market without the overhead of generalization
