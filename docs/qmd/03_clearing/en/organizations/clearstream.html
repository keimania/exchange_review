2. Settlement Model

2.1 Clearstream Banking S.A. (CBL – ICSD)

As one of two global ICSDs, CBL provides settlement and custody for international securities (primarily Eurobonds) and cross-border transactions:

  • Book-Entry Settlement: All securities held in dematerialized or immobilized form; transfers through book entries
  • Multi-Currency Settlement: Settles in multiple currencies with commercial bank money (CMB) via correspondent banking relationships
  • Internal Settlement: Transactions between two CBL participants settle internally on CBL’s books
  • Bridge Settlement: Transactions between CBL and Euroclear Bank participants settle via the Bridge (see Section 7)
  • External Settlement: Transactions involving securities held at other CSDs settle through CBL’s network of links

2.2 Clearstream Europe AG (CSD – Germany + Pan-European)

Clearstream Europe (formerly CBF) serves as: - German Domestic CSD: Settlement and custody for all German domestic securities - Pan-European CSD: Via T2S, provides access to 19 European markets through a single point of entry - T2S Settlement: Uses central bank money (EUR) via TARGET2 for the cash leg; real-time gross settlement (RTGS) - Over 50% of T2S Volume: Clearstream Europe consistently handles more than half of all T2S settlement traffic

3. Risk Management

3.1 Margin Framework

Clearstream (as a CSD/ICSD) does not operate as a CCP and does not collect traditional margins. Risk management focuses on:

  • DVP Settlement: Simultaneous exchange of securities and cash eliminates principal risk
  • Credit Facilities: CBL provides credit extensions to participants secured by pledged collateral
  • Collateral Valuation: Securities used as collateral are marked-to-market daily with conservative haircuts
  • Exposure Limits: Participant-specific credit and exposure limits monitored in real time
  • Liquidity Risk Management: Cash forecasting and management of settlement liquidity across currencies

3.3 Default Waterfall

As a CSD/ICSD (not CCP), Clearstream does not maintain a mutualized default fund. Risk absorption follows:

  1. Defaulter’s pledged collateral – CBL has the right to liquidate securities pledged by the defaulting participant
  2. Clearstream’s own capital – capital buffers maintained under CRR/CRD requirements
  3. No loss mutualization – non-defaulting participants are not called upon to absorb losses

3.6 Recovery & Resolution

  • CSDR Framework: Subject to EU CSD Recovery and Resolution requirements
  • Banking Resolution: CBL, as a licensed credit institution, is subject to the Bank Recovery and Resolution Directive (BRRD)
  • Resolution Authority: CSSF (Luxembourg) for CBL; BaFin/SRB for Clearstream Europe AG
  • Systemic Importance: Designated as systemically important financial market infrastructure
  • Banking License Review (2025): Deutsche Borse Group is reviewing whether Clearstream Europe AG needs to retain its banking license under the amended CSDR; CBL will continue to leverage its CRR license as the Service CSD of the group

5. Technology & Infrastructure

  • CASCADE / Creation: Core settlement and custody systems for German domestic and international securities
  • T2S Integration: Full integration with the Eurosystem’s T2S platform since 2017 for pan-European settlement
  • Collateral Management System (OneCMS): Unified collateral management across ICSD and CSD locations, enabling optimized collateral allocation
  • D7 Platform: Deutsche Borse Group’s digital post-trade platform for DLT-based securities issuance and custody [unverified – in development]
  • Crypto-Asset Custody: CBL authorized by CSSF as crypto-asset service provider (2025), extending regulated custody to digital assets
  • Eurobond Digitization: Joint initiative with Euroclear to support dematerialized Eurobond issuance from Q1 2026

7. Interoperability

7.1 The Bridge (Clearstream-Euroclear)

The Bridge is the critical interoperability link between the two global ICSDs:

  • Purpose: Enables settlement of securities transactions between Clearstream Banking Luxembourg and Euroclear Bank participants
  • Settlement: Both free-of-payment and delivery-versus-payment transactions
  • Processing: Multiple overnight batch exchanges plus Automated Daytime Bridge for real-time settlement during business hours
  • DVP Model: Model 1 (simultaneous, final transfer)
  • CSDR Status: The Bridge link is explicitly covered under CBL’s CSDR authorization
  • History: Operational since the 1970s; continuously modernized

7.2 CSD Links Network

Clearstream maintains an extensive global network of CSD links:

  • Clearstream Europe: Direct T2S links to 19 European markets; additional links to non-T2S markets
  • CBL (ICSD): Links to CSDs and agent banks providing access to 60+ markets globally
  • Cross-CSD Settlement: T2S enables harmonized cross-CSD settlement between Clearstream Europe and other T2S-connected CSDs (including Euroclear group CSDs)

7.3 T2S Participation

7.4 Global Securities Financing (GSF) Integration

Clearstream’s interoperability extends to collateral and lending services: - Harmonized GSF across locations: Since T2S migration in 2017, GSF services (securities lending, collateral management) are available across both ICSD and CSD positions - Single Pool: Clients can pool assets from ICSD and CSD (T2S) accounts for lending and collateral purposes - Fails Lending on T2S: Automated Securities Lending (ASL) service covers T2S settlement, providing the same service level as the ICSD

Key Strategic Themes

  1. Pan-European CSD Consolidation: Clearstream Europe serves as a gateway to 19 European markets via T2S, aiming to become the pan-European CSD of choice
  2. Regulatory Simplification: Potential return of Clearstream Europe’s banking license under CSDR refit, reducing regulatory overlap while CBL retains its CRR license for the group
  3. Digital Assets: Expansion into crypto-asset custody (CASP license) and DLT-based collateral services
  4. Eurobond Modernization: Joint digitization initiative with Euroclear to eliminate paper certificates
  5. Global Liquidity Hub: Deepening integration of collateral management, securities lending, and financing services across ICSD and CSD locations
  6. T+1 Readiness: Preparing for EU transition to T+1 settlement cycle
