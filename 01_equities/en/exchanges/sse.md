### 2.4 Lot Sizes
- **Main Board:** Standard lot = 100 shares; buy orders must be in multiples of 100 shares; sell orders for odd lots (<100 shares) must be sold in a single order; maximum order size = 1,000,000 shares
- **STAR Market:** Minimum order = 200 shares for both limit and market orders; limit order max = 100,000 shares; market order max = 50,000 shares; odd lots (<200 shares) must be sold in a single order

## 3. Trading Mechanism

### 3.1 Auction Mechanisms

**Opening Call Auction (9:15-9:30):**
- Orders collected during 9:15-9:25 pre-open period
- System calculates a single price that maximizes executable volume at 9:25
- Price determination principle: the price at which the maximum volume of shares can be traded; if multiple prices satisfy this condition, the price closest to the previous close is selected
- Unmatched orders carry forward to continuous trading session
- Orders submitted between 9:20-9:25 cannot be cancelled (to prevent manipulation)

**Closing Call Auction (14:57-15:00):**
- Replaces continuous trading for the final 3 minutes of the session
- Same single-price auction principle as opening auction
- Orders accepted but cannot be cancelled during this period
- Determines the official closing price of each security
- Applicable to both Main Board and STAR Market

**STAR Market After-hours Fixed-price Trading (15:05-15:30):**
- All trades executed at the day's closing price
- Time priority for order matching
- Provides additional liquidity window for STAR Market stocks

### 3.2 Continuous Trading
- **Order-driven market:** Pure order-driven (no designated market makers for Main Board equities)
- **Price-time priority:** Orders are matched first by best price, then by time of entry at the same price level
- Buy orders at the highest price and sell orders at the lowest price are matched first
- Trade execution price is determined by existing resting orders (passive side)

### 3.3 Price Discovery
- Opening price: Determined by opening call auction; if no trades occur during auction, the first trade during continuous trading becomes the opening price
- Closing price: Determined by closing call auction (14:57-15:00)
- **T+1 trading rule** significantly affects intraday price discovery -- shares purchased on day T cannot be sold until day T+1, reducing intraday liquidity and creating asymmetric trading patterns (investors can buy shares they sold earlier the same day, but cannot sell shares purchased the same day)

## 4. Order Types

### 4.1 Standard Order Types
| Order Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Limit Order (éä»·å§æ)** | Buy or sell at a specified price or better; accepted during both auction and continuous trading sessions |
| **Market Order** | Main Board: two variants available (see below); only during continuous trading; not accepted during auction sessions |

### 4.2 Market Order Variants (Main Board)
| Order Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Five Best Orders Immediate or Cancel** | Executes against the best 5 price levels on the opposite side; unmatched remainder is automatically cancelled |
| **Five Best Orders Immediate to Limit** | Executes against the best 5 price levels on the opposite side; unmatched remainder converts to a limit order at the last executed price |

### 4.3 STAR Market Additional Order Types
| Order Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Best Counterparty Order** | Executes at the best price on the opposite side [unverified] |
| **Best Own-side Order** | Enters as a limit order at the best price on the same side [unverified] |
| **Five Best + Cancel / Five Best + Limit** | Same as Main Board market order variants |

### 4.4 Time-in-Force Options
| TIF | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| DAY | Default; order valid for the current trading day only |
| IOC (Immediate or Cancel) | Implicit in the "Five Best Immediate or Cancel" market order type |

**Note:** GTC (Good Till Cancelled), FOK (Fill or Kill), and other advanced TIF options commonly found on Western exchanges are generally **not available** on SSE [unverified]. The limited TIF options reflect the simpler order type structure of Chinese exchanges.

### 4.5 Order Modification / Cancellation
- Orders may be cancelled during the opening pre-open period (9:15-9:20) but not during 9:20-9:25
- Orders cannot be cancelled during the closing call auction (14:57-15:00)
- During continuous trading, unexecuted and partially executed orders may be cancelled
- Order modification is generally implemented as cancel-and-replace

## 8. Risk Controls & Circuit Breakers

### 8.1 Price Limits
| Market Segment | Daily Price Limit | Notes |
|----------------|-------------------|-------|
| Main Board (A-shares) | +/-10% from previous close | Applied to normal stocks; calculated based on previous trading day's closing price |
| Main Board (ST / *ST stocks) | +/-5% from previous close | Special Treatment (ST) and delisting risk (*ST) stocks have tighter limits |
| STAR Market | +/-20% from previous close | Wider limits to accommodate higher-growth, higher-volatility tech stocks |
| STAR Market (first 5 trading days) | No price limit | New IPO listings have no price limit for the first 5 days of trading |
| B-shares | +/-10% from previous close | Same as Main Board A-shares |

### 8.2 Circuit Breakers
| Status | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Currently: No market-wide circuit breaker** | China attempted an index-based circuit breaker mechanism in January 2016 but suspended it after only 4 trading days |

**2016 Circuit Breaker Episode:**
- **Implemented:** January 1, 2016, based on the CSI 300 Index
- **5% decline threshold:** Trading halted for 15 minutes; if triggered after 14:45, trading halted for remainder of day
- **7% decline threshold:** Trading halted for remainder of the day
- **Triggered:** January 4, 2016 (both 5% and 7% thresholds hit) and January 7, 2016 (both thresholds hit within minutes)
- **Suspended:** January 8, 2016 -- CSRC suspended the mechanism permanently
- **Reason for failure:** The circuit breaker created a "magnet effect" -- investors rushed to sell before being locked in by the halt, creating a self-fulfilling prophecy that accelerated the decline. The proximity of the 5% and 7% thresholds (~2% gap) gave insufficient buffer time
- **Current status:** Individual stock price limits (+/-10% / +/-20%) remain the primary volatility control mechanism; no plans to reintroduce market-wide circuit breakers as of 2026

### 8.3 Pre-trade Risk Controls
- **Price limit validation:** Orders outside the daily price limit band are rejected by the system
- **Order size limits:** Maximum order size of 1,000,000 shares (Main Board) / 100,000 shares for STAR Market limit orders / 50,000 shares for STAR Market market orders
- **Abnormal trading monitoring:** SSE monitors for abnormal trading patterns including frequent cancellations, large orders intended to manipulate, and coordinated trading [unverified -- specific thresholds not publicly disclosed]
- **Broker-level risk controls:** Member brokers required to implement their own pre-trade risk checks per CSRC regulations

### 8.4 Kill Switch
- SSE has authority to suspend trading in individual securities or halt market-wide trading under extraordinary circumstances
- CSRC may direct SSE to implement emergency measures
- Specific kill switch technical capabilities not publicly documented in English sources

## 9. Regulatory Framework

### 9.1 Supervising Authority
- **China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC):** Primary regulator of China's securities and futures markets; equivalent to the U.S. SEC
- SSE is directly supervised by CSRC since 1997 (by State Council directive)
- SSE also functions as a **front-line self-regulatory organization (SRO)**, conducting listing review, trading surveillance, and member supervision under CSRC delegation
- **State Council of the PRC:** Ultimate authority over capital market policy

### 9.2 Key Regulations
| Regulation | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Securities Law of the PRC (2019 revision)** | Fundamental law governing securities issuance, trading, and regulation; introduced registration-based IPO system framework |
| **Company Law of the PRC** | Governs corporate governance requirements for listed companies |
| **SSE Trading Rules (2023 Revision)** | Detailed rules for order submission, execution, settlement, and market conduct |
| **SSE STAR Market Listing Rules** | Specific requirements for STAR Market IPOs under registration-based system |
| **Measures for the Administration of Stock Exchanges** | CSRC rules on exchange governance and operations |
| **Administrative Measures for QFI** | Unified rules for foreign institutional investor access (merged QFII/RQFII in 2020) |

### 9.3 Market Surveillance
- SSE operates real-time market surveillance systems monitoring for insider trading, market manipulation, and abnormal trading
- Coordinated surveillance with CSRC and China Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation (CSDC)
- SSE has authority to issue warnings, restrict trading, and refer cases to CSRC for enforcement
- Cross-market surveillance coordination with Shenzhen Stock Exchange for cross-listed securities and index products

### 9.4 Reporting Requirements
- Listed companies must comply with continuous disclosure obligations (annual, semi-annual, quarterly reports)
- Major shareholders must report holdings changes (5% disclosure threshold)
- Short selling and securities lending reporting requirements
- Connected/related party transaction disclosure

### 9.5 Cross-border Arrangements

**Stock Connect (Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect):**
- **Launched:** November 17, 2014
- **Northbound:** International investors trade SSE-listed A-shares via HKEX brokers
- **Southbound:** Mainland investors trade HKEX-listed stocks via SSE member brokers
- **Eligible stocks:** Large/mid-cap A-shares (SSE 180, SSE 380 constituents, and dual-listed A+H stocks); scope has been gradually expanded
- **Daily quota:** Northbound daily quota of RMB 52 billion (expanded from original RMB 13 billion); aggregate quota abolished in 2018
- **Settlement:** T+0 for Northbound (trades settle same day at HKSCC); underlying A-shares follow T+1 trading rule
- **ETF Connect:** Expanded to include eligible ETFs since July 2022

**Qualified Foreign Investor (QFI) Scheme:**
- Merged from QFII (2002) and RQFII (2011) into unified QFI scheme in 2020
- Investment quotas abolished in 2020 -- no cap on investment amount
- Requires CSRC license and SAFE registration
- Provides broader access than Stock Connect (bonds, derivatives, private placement, etc.)
- As of 2025, CSRC continues to optimize the QFI regime for further market opening

**A-shares / B-shares Classification:**
| Share Type | Currency | Investor Access | Status |
|-----------|----------|----------------|--------|
| A-shares | RMB | Domestic investors, QFI holders, Stock Connect participants | Primary market; vast majority of listings and volume |
| B-shares | USD (SSE) / HKD (SZSE) | Originally foreign-only; opened to domestic investors in 2001 | Legacy market; minimal new activity; periodic discussions about B-share reform or phase-out |

## China-Specific Features Summary

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **T+1 Trading Rule** | Shares purchased on day T cannot be sold until day T+1; this is asymmetric -- shares sold on day T can be repurchased the same day. This fundamentally limits day-trading and affects market microstructure |
| **Daily Price Limits** | +/-10% Main Board / +/-20% STAR Market replace Western-style circuit breakers as the primary volatility mechanism |
| **State Ownership** | SSE is a state-owned, non-profit institution directly supervised by CSRC -- unlike most major global exchanges which are publicly listed, for-profit corporations |
| **A/B Share Dual Structure** | Legacy system of dual share classes by currency/investor type; B-shares largely obsolete but still traded |
| **Foreign Access Restrictions** | Foreign investors cannot trade A-shares directly; access only through QFI scheme or Stock Connect; strategic/sensitive sectors may have additional restrictions |
| **Registration vs. Approval IPO** | STAR Market uses registration-based system (similar to Western markets); Main Board historically used approval-based system but is transitioning to registration |
| **CSRC Dominant Role** | CSRC exercises much more direct control over exchange operations, IPO approvals, and market interventions than Western regulators typically do |
| **Stock Connect Mechanism** | Unique cross-border trading link with HKEX that enables foreign access while maintaining China's capital controls |
