#### GC Pooling

GC Pooling is the European benchmark for standardised secured funding with central clearing. Key features:

- Pre-defined baskets (e.g., GC Pooling ECB Basket covering ~3,000 ECB-eligible securities)
- Automated connectivity to Eurex Clearing AG (CCP) and Clearstream Banking (settlement)
- Available in EUR, USD, GBP, and CHF
- More than 160 sell-side and buy-side institutions participate
- Re-use of received collateral permitted for further money market transactions, Eurex Clearing margining, and ECB open market operations
- Operating since 2005

Source: [Eurex GC Pooling](https://www.eurex.com/ex-en/markets/eurex-repo/gc-pooling)

#### Lending CCP

The Lending CCP provides centrally cleared securities lending with distinctive features:

- Eurex Clearing acts as single counterparty to all trades, eliminating bilateral credit risk
- **Specific Lender License**: Direct CCP access for beneficial owners (pension funds, asset managers) without requiring full clearing membership
- Near-time intraday risk calculations with intraday margin calls for collateral shortfalls
- Triparty collateral agents connected for non-cash collateral management
- Covers international fixed income, European equities, and ETFs
- Partnership with Pirum for post-trade automation; integration with EquiLend for trade sourcing

Sources: [Eurex Lending CCP](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/find/news/Clearstream-Eurex-Repo-and-Eurex-Clearing-offer-clients-joint-agency-securities-lending-141856); [Eurex Clearing Lending CCP Enhancements](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/find/news/Eurex-Clearing-enhances-the-marketplace-for-securities-lending-central-clearing-155016)

### 2.2 Novation Process

- **Novation by open offer**: For exchange-traded products on Eurex Exchange and Frankfurt Stock Exchange, Eurex Clearing interposes itself as the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer at the point of trade execution.
- **Novation for OTC products**: For OTC IRS and repo transactions, the bilateral trade is replaced by two separate contracts with Eurex Clearing as CCP upon trade registration/acceptance.
- **Straight-through processing**: GC Pooling and repo clearing provide fully automated STP from trade execution through CCP clearing to settlement at Clearstream.

### 2.3 Netting

- **Multilateral netting**: Eurex Clearing performs multilateral netting across all cleared positions, reducing gross exposures to a single net obligation per clearing member per product/settlement date.
- **Portfolio compression**: For OTC IRS, compression services reduce the number of line items and gross notional outstanding.
- **Prisma cross-margining netting**: Within liquidation groups, Prisma provides portfolio-level netting that recognizes hedging and diversification effects across correlated products.

## 3. Risk Management

### 3.1 Margin Framework: Eurex Clearing Prisma

**Eurex Clearing Prisma** (Portfolio-based Risk management by Simulating scenario Matrices and Assessing market risks) is Eurex Clearing's proprietary portfolio-based margin methodology, introduced in 2012.

#### Core Methodology

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Model Type | Filtered Historical Simulation (FHS) with Value-at-Risk (VaR) |
| Confidence Level | 99% for listed products; 99.5% for OTC instruments |
| Effective Confidence Level | Exceeds target levels (verified via backtesting) |
| Historical Scenarios | 750 filtered-historical scenarios with 3-year lookback period |
| Stress Scenarios | 250 stress period scenarios from identified historical stress episodes |
| Event Risk Scenarios | Supplementary scenarios for specific risk factors (e.g., single stock dividend futures) |
| Holding Period -- Listed Equity Derivatives | 2-3 days (PEQ01 liquidation group reduced from 4 to 3 days; subsequently to 2 days for some) |
| Holding Period -- Listed Fixed Income | 2 days (PFI01_HP2 split) |
| Holding Period -- OTC IRS | 5 days (PFI01_HP5 split) |
| Risk Measure | Maximum of VaR from filtered historical sample and VaR from stress period sample |
| Procyclicality Mitigation | Stress period VaR serves as prudent margin floor during low-volatility regimes; annual recalibration of margin floors |

Sources: [Eurex Clearing Prisma](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/services/margining/eurex-clearing-prisma); [ECC Prisma for Commodities](https://www.ecc.de/en/risk-management/ecc-prisma-for-commodities); [Part 8: Forward Looking Margin Simulations](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/find/news/Part-8-Forward-looking-margin-simulations-into-periods-of-stress-3193710)

#### Margin Components

Prisma initial margin consists of multiple components:

1. **Market Risk Initial Margin (MRIM)**: Core risk component based on filtered historical simulation; captures potential portfolio value changes under historical and stress scenarios
2. **Liquidity Adjustment (LA)**: Accounts for the cost of liquidating concentrated positions; adjusts for bid-ask spread widening and market impact during portfolio close-out
3. **KnockIn / Event Risk Component**: Additional margin for specific risk events (e.g., barrier events for structured products, dividend event risk for single stock derivatives)

The backward-looking component (filtered historical VaR) ensures model reactivity to changing market conditions, while the forward-looking component (stress period VaR) ensures margin stability and counters procyclicality.

#### Liquidation Groups

Products are organized into Liquidation Groups based on shared risk characteristics. Key liquidation groups include:

| Liquidation Group | Products | Holding Period |
|-------------------|----------|---------------|
| **PFI01** (Fixed Income) | Listed fixed income futures/options + OTC IRS | Split: HP2 (2-day) for listed; HP5 (5-day) for OTC |
| **PEQ01** (Equity) | Listed equity/index derivatives | 2-3 days |
| **FX Liquidation Group** | OTC FX NDFs | 2 days [unverified] |
| **Repo/GC Pooling** | Repo and secured funding (currently under Risk-Based Margining; migrating to Prisma Bonds from December 2025) | Varies by product |

Source: [Eurex Clearing Prisma Circular on PEQ01](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/find/circulars/Eurex-Clearing-Prisma-Reduction-of-the-risk-horizon-for-Equity-Liquidation-Group-PEQ01-228482); [Prisma Bonds Circular](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/find/circulars/clearing-circular-4609908)

#### Cross-Margining

Cross-margining is a distinctive feature of Prisma, enabling margin offsets between products with different holding periods:

- **Listed-OTC Cross-Margining**: Within PFI01, cross-margining is offered between OTC IRS (5-day holding period) and listed fixed income products (2-day holding period)
- **Algorithm**: The system checks whether listed FI/MM positions are economically sensible hedges to the OTC portfolio; if so, these positions are moved to the 5-day split to lower total margin
- **Margin Efficiency**: Potential margin savings of **up to 70%** depending on portfolio composition
- **Advantage over SPAN**: Unlike CME's SPAN methodology (which calculates margin product-by-product with limited inter-commodity spreads), Prisma assesses risk at the portfolio level using full revaluation under historical scenarios, enabling more accurate capture of hedging benefits

Source: [Eurex Clearing Cross-Margining](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/services/margining/cross-margining)

#### Comparison with Other Margin Methodologies

| Feature | Eurex Clearing Prisma | CME SPAN | OCC STANS |
|---------|----------------------|----------|-----------|
| Approach | Portfolio-based filtered historical simulation | Scenario-based (16 risk arrays per product) | Monte Carlo simulation |
| Risk Measure | VaR (max of filtered historical and stress period) | Worst-case loss across scanning scenarios | Expected Shortfall (ES) |
| Confidence Level | 99% (listed) / 99.5% (OTC) | ~99% (varies by exchange) | 99.5% (2-day, ES) |
| Cross-Margining | Yes, within liquidation groups across listed and OTC | Limited inter-commodity credits | Portfolio margining across equity products |
| Scenarios | 750 historical + 250 stress = 1,000 total | 16 scanning scenarios per product | Full Monte Carlo revaluation |
| Procyclicality | Stress period VaR floor | APC (Anti-Procyclicality) measures | Floor based on historical averages |

### 3.3 Stress Testing & Back-Testing

| Requirement | Implementation |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Stress Testing** | Daily stress testing of all clearing member portfolios against extreme but plausible scenarios; includes historical scenarios (e.g., Lehman 2008, COVID-19 2020, Ukraine crisis 2022) and hypothetical scenarios |

### 3.4 Recovery & Resolution

Eurex Clearing operates under the **EU CCP Recovery and Resolution Regulation** (Regulation (EU) 2021/23), fully applicable since 12 February 2023.

**Recovery Tools:**
- **Variation Margin Gains Haircutting (VMGH)**: Reduction of VM payments to non-defaulting members to cover uncovered losses
- **Cash calls / Assessments**: Non-defaulting members can be required to contribute additional funds (up to 2x their Default Fund contribution)
- **Position allocation / Forced allocation**: Transfer or termination of the defaulting member's portfolio
- **Partial tear-up**: Termination of contracts where necessary to restore matched book

**Resolution Tools (BaFin as Resolution Authority):**
- **Resolution Cash Calls**: BaFin can require non-defaulting members to provide further contributions up to 2x their applicable contribution requirement
- **Write-down and conversion tools**: Authority to write down liabilities or convert claims
- **Position and loss allocation tools**: BaFin can allocate positions and losses
- **Transfer tools**: Transfer of clearing member positions to a bridge CCP or another CCP

Clearing members are contractually bound by any resolution tools taken by BaFin, including those affecting their assets, rights, obligations, and liabilities.

Source: [Eurex Clearing CCP RRR Circular](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/find/circulars/clearing-circular-3140368)

### 4.3 Settlement Finality

- **Legal basis**: German law (specifically the German implementation of the EU Settlement Finality Directive 98/26/EC)
- **EU Settlement Finality Directive (SFD)**: Eurex Clearing AG is a designated system under the SFD, ensuring that transfer orders entered into the system are legally enforceable and irrevocable even in the event of insolvency of a participant
- **Point of finality on T2S**: Determined by the General Terms and Conditions of Clearstream Europe AG; once a settlement instruction is matched and settled on T2S, the transfer is final and irrevocable
- **Central bank money finality**: Settlement in EUR via T2 provides central bank money finality under Eurosystem rules

### 8.4 Membership Fees

### 8.5 Prisma Margin Netting Benefits

The Prisma portfolio-based margining approach provides significant economic benefits to clearing members:
- **Cross-margining savings**: Up to 70% margin reduction for portfolios with offsetting listed FI and OTC IRS positions
- **Portfolio margining**: Full netting within liquidation groups, recognizing hedging and correlation effects
- **Capital efficiency**: More accurate risk assessment leads to lower capital requirements under Basel III/CRD IV compared to standardized or bilateral approaches
- **Single collateral pool**: One margin pool across listed and OTC positions reduces operational complexity and collateral fragmentation
